DEBRIEF Graduate Exhibition 2026

Otago Polytechnic's, School of Design annual end-of-year exhibition for all graduating students. Creating something vibrant, unique and student-focused was key to this project.

Shopify Store: Maxis Design

Our task was to create a site using Shopify - anything we wanted. I had wanted to create a website for myself and work earlier in the year, and thought this course could be a great opportunity to trial different design choices and test a store-version of my work.

Tahi Otepoti Festival 2026

A new solo performance festival brought to Dunedin. A collaboration between the Dunedin Arts Festival crew and the Tahi (wellington) team. They were requiring a brand identity, posters, brochure, social media and website help to bring the event to life.

Tuhura Museum Shop

The Tuhura Otago Museum was revamping their store shop. Looking to the School of Design students for help, we were to tasks to pitch, plan, draft, iterate and create different store items which could potentially be used within the museum store.

No Money, No Skill, No Problem!

This project combined Fashion and Communication design students to create any item for sale in a market day. Our item of choice was a cookbook which GENUINLEY targetted University students, only consisting of meals which had actually affordable and easily accessable ingredients and were either easy or quick to cook up.

Broad Bay School Rebrand

The small suburb of Broad Bay School was needing a rebrand to encourage new enrolment and have a stronger visual identity and presence.
